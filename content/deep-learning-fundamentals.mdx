## 🗺️ What this series covers

Deep learning has transformed how we solve complex problems in vision, language, and beyond. This series walks through the foundations — from how a single neuron works, to the architectures powering today's AI models.

---

## 📚 Chapters

### [Chapter 1 — Introduction to Deep Learning](/blog/deep-learning-1-introduction)

The AI → ML → Deep Learning hierarchy. Biological neurons vs artificial neurons. Forward propagation and activation functions.

### [Chapter 2 — Training Deep Networks](/blog/deep-learning-2-basics)

Gradient descent and its variants. Backpropagation step by step. Loss functions, the vanishing gradient problem, and a full activation function reference.

### [Chapter 3 — Deep Learning with Keras](/blog/deep-learning-3-keras)

TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Keras compared. Building a regression model and a classification model from scratch — with code walkthroughs.

### [Chapter 4 — Deep Learning Models](/blog/deep-learning-4-models)

CNNs for images, RNNs and LSTMs for sequences, Transformers and the attention mechanism, Autoencoders and RBMs.
